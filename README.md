## 👋 About Me

I'm **Kaleb Kebede**, a Software Engineering student at **Wachemo University** who builds complete products, from polished frontends and solid backend APIs to mobile apps and AI-powered automations.

- 🚀 Building full-stack web apps with **React**, **Next.js**, **Node.js** and **Express**
- 📱 Crafting modern mobile apps with **Kotlin** and **Flutter**
- 🤖 Designing AI agents and automated workflows with **n8n** and **LangGraph**
- 🐳 Shipping consistently with **Docker**
- 🌱 I learn by building real projects, not just following tutorials
- 🤝 Open to collaboration, freelance work and open-source
